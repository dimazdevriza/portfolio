# Design Direction: Dimaz Devriza Portfolio

## 1. Identity, Character & Target Audience
- **Target Audience**: Technical Recruiters, Engineering Managers, and Technical Leads looking for a high-capability Full-Stack Web Developer.
- **Brand Personality**: Engineered, disciplined, transparent, and refined. A systems builder who understands database atomicity (ACID), networking infrastructure (MTCNA), and clean modern web architectures (Laravel, Livewire, Alpine, Astro).
- **Core Value Statement**: Full-stack developer with real-world production experience designing concurrent logistics and educational systems, backed by certified networking and cloud AI foundations.

---

## 2. The Three Dials (Liveliness Calibration)
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)**
  Clean, high-credibility presentation. Strong typographical presence and crisp borders without chaotic clutter or noisy AI mesh gradients.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Varied Narrative Rhythm)**
  Breaks out of uniform card grids. Uses an asymmetrical split hero, dedicated full-width case study breakdowns with architectural flowcharts, and a compact credential verification grid.
- **MOTION: 2.5 (Fluid Tactile Physics)**
  Apple-inspired fluid interactions: frosted glass navigation bar, spring-eased entry staggers, smooth scroll reveals, and responsive tactile button presses (`active:scale-[0.97]`). Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 3. Visual Language & Color Palette (R-29)
Restrained palette focusing on optical clarity and depth:
- **Base Canvas (Dark Theme primary)**:
  - Deep Obsidian Canvas: `#090D16`
  - Elevated Card Background: `#111827` (with `border-slate-800/80`)
  - Frosted Glass Surface: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)` with `backdrop-blur-xl` and `border-white/10`
- **Text & Foreground (Meeting WCAG AA contrast R-25)**:
  - High Emphasis Text: `#F9FAFB` (Pure light slate, > 14:1 contrast ratio)
  - Secondary / Body Text: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400, > 5.5:1 contrast ratio)
  - Muted Technical Meta: `#64748B` (Slate 500, used for tags and timestamps)
- **One Deliberate Accent (Apple / Azure Blue)**:
  - Primary Accent: `#38BDF8` (Sky 400) / `#2563EB` (Royal Blue)
  - Subtle interactive glow / ring: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`
- **Border Radii (R-11)**:
  - Outer card containers: `rounded-2xl` (16px)
  - Interactive pills and buttons: `rounded-xl` (12px)
  - Small technical badges: `rounded-md` (6px)

---

## 4. Typography (R-06)
- **Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (crisp geometric proportions, modern authority)
- **Body Text**: `Inter`, sans-serif (exceptional legibility at all font sizes)
- **Code & Architecture Indicators**: `JetBrains Mono` or `ui-monospace` (precise, legible technical metadata)

---

## 5. Architectural Case Study Presentation (R-05, R-14, R-38)
Instead of generic mockup cards, feature Dimaz's actual production systems:
1. **D'Royal Village Multi-Warehouse Logistics & Cost Control**:
   - Focus: Concurrency protection, pessimistic database locking (`lockForUpdate`), multi-role access (Logistics, Supervisor, Finance, Admin), and material cost tracking to individual housing units.

2. **SMA Negeri 15 Padang LMS**:
   - Focus: Real school-wide adoption, automated grading workflows, Git deployment to hosting infrastructure.
3. **LLDIKTI Wilayah X SiLAB**:
